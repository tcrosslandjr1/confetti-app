# Confetti Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 19, 2026
**Entity:** Confetti App, LLC

---

## 1. Introduction

Confetti App, LLC ("we," "us," "our") operates the Confetti mobile application and website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- Account details (name, email, phone, date of birth)
- Profile preferences (cuisine, ambiance, dietary restrictions)
- Reviews, ratings, and feedback
- Payment information (processed by third-party payment processors)
- Communications with our support team or AI concierge

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Device identifiers (device ID, OS version, app version)
- Location data (with your permission)
- Usage data (pages viewed, features used, session duration)
- Log data (IP address, browser type, access times)

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- Social media profile data (when you connect accounts)
- Venue booking confirmations (with your permission)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and personalize the Service
- Build and maintain your Taste Graph (see Data Profiling Addendum, Doc 17)
- Process transactions and send billing communications
- Send service-related notifications
- Improve our AI recommendation models
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

- **Consent:** Taste Graph profiling, marketing communications, special category data
- **Contract Performance:** Account management, service delivery
- **Legitimate Interest:** Service improvement, fraud prevention, analytics
- **Legal Obligation:** Tax reporting, law enforcement requests

## 5. AI Profiling & Automated Decision-Making

Confetti uses AI to build personalized Taste Graphs. See the Data Profiling & Taste Learning Addendum (Doc 17) for complete details on how profiling works, your GDPR Article 22 rights, and your controls.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We share data with:
- **Service Providers:** Hosting, analytics, payment processing, email delivery
- **Venue Partners:** Anonymized and aggregated trend data only
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, subpoena, or court order
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

## 7. Cookies & Tracking

See the Cookie & Tracking Consent Policy (Doc 03) for details on cookies, pixels, and analytics tools.

## 8. Data Retention

- Account data: Duration of account + 30 days after deletion
- Taste Graph: Active account + 30 days (or until opt-out)
- Behavioral signals: 90 days rolling
- Payment records: 7 years (tax/legal compliance)
- Support communications: 3 years

## 9. Your Rights

### GDPR (EU/EEA/UK)
- Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority

### CCPA (California)
- Right to know, delete, opt-out of sale (we do not sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination

### All Users
- Access your data: Profile > Privacy > Download My Data
- Delete your data: Profile > Privacy > Delete My Account
- Manage preferences: Profile > Taste Settings

## 10. International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and other safeguards for EU/EEA/UK data transfers.

## 11. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit
- AES-256 encryption at rest
- Access controls and audit logging
- Regular security assessments and penetration testing

## 12. Children's Privacy

Confetti does not knowingly collect data from children under 18. See the Age Verification Gate Policy (Doc 06).

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification at least 30 days before the effective date.
