# Confetti Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** May 19, 2026
**Entity:** Confetti App, LLC

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules and boundaries for using the Confetti Service. Violations may result in account suspension or termination.

## 2. Prohibited Activities

You may NOT use Confetti to:

### Content Violations
- Post false reviews, ratings, or misleading venue information
- Upload sexually explicit, violent, or illegal content
- Post content that infringes third-party intellectual property
- Engage in hate speech, harassment, or discrimination

### Technical Violations
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Service
- Use automated bots, scrapers, or crawlers
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to any system or account
- Introduce malware, viruses, or other harmful code
- Circumvent rate limits, access controls, or security measures

### Commercial Violations
- Use the Service for unauthorized commercial purposes
- Resell, sublicense, or redistribute Confetti content
- Solicit users for competing services
- Engage in affiliate fraud or fake referrals

### Legal Violations
- Violate any applicable local, state, national, or international law
- Facilitate illegal gambling, drug use, or underage drinking
- Engage in money laundering or fraud
- Violate export control or sanctions regulations

## 3. Enforcement

Violations are handled as follows:
- **First offense:** Warning with explanation
- **Second offense:** 7-day account suspension
- **Third offense:** Permanent account termination
- **Severe violations:** Immediate termination (no warnings)

We reserve the right to remove any content and suspend any account at our sole discretion.

## 4. Reporting

Report AUP violations to abuse@confetti.app. Include screenshots or details where possible. All reports are reviewed within 48 hours.
