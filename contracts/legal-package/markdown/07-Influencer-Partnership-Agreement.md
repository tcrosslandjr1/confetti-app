# Confetti Influencer Partnership Agreement

**Effective Date:** May 19, 2026
**Entity:** Confetti App, LLC

---

## 1. Parties

This Influencer Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Confetti App, LLC ("Company") and the Influencer identified in the signature block below ("Influencer").

## 2. Engagement & Scope

The Company engages the Influencer as an independent contractor to create and distribute content promoting Confetti across social media platforms, blogs, and other digital channels as mutually agreed.

## 3. Influencer Tiers & Compensation

| Tier | Follower Range | Base Fee | Rev Share | Bonus |
|------|---------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Micro | 5K - 25K | $100 - $500/post | 3% referral | Venue credit |
| Mid-Tier | 25K - 100K | $500 - $2,000/post | 5% referral | Venue credit + events |
| Macro | 100K - 500K | $2,000 - $10,000/post | 7% referral | Full VIP package |
| Celebrity | 500K+ | Custom negotiation | 10% referral | Custom package |

## 4. Content Requirements

- All content must include proper FTC disclosure (#ad, #sponsored, or equivalent)
- Content must be original and not infringe third-party rights
- Brand assets must be used according to the Confetti Brand Guidelines
- Company reserves the right to request revisions within 48 hours of submission
- Minimum content quality standards as outlined in the Content Brief

## 5. Exclusivity

During the term, Influencer agrees not to promote competing dining/nightlife recommendation platforms. General restaurant or bar content is permitted.

## 6. Intellectual Property

- Influencer retains ownership of original content
- Company receives a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license per the Content License Agreement (Doc 10)
- Company may repurpose content for marketing with attribution

## 7. Confidentiality

Influencer agrees to the Influencer NDA (Doc 09) regarding proprietary information including unreleased features, partnership terms, and internal data.

## 8. Term & Termination

- **Initial Term:** 6 months from the Effective Date
- **Renewal:** Auto-renews for successive 3-month terms unless either party gives 30 days' written notice
- **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate immediately for material breach
- **Post-Termination:** Content remains licensed; unpublished content rights revert to Influencer

## 9. Representations & Warranties

Influencer represents that:
- They have the legal authority to enter this Agreement
- Content will comply with all applicable laws and FTC guidelines
- They will disclose any conflicts of interest
- Follower counts and engagement metrics are accurate and not artificially inflated

## 10. Dispute Resolution

Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the AAA in Washington, DC. Governing law: District of Columbia.
