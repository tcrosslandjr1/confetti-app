# Confetti Influencer Payment Schedule Addendum

**Effective Date:** May 19, 2026
**Entity:** Confetti App, LLC

---

## 1. Scope

This Payment Schedule Addendum supplements the Influencer Partnership Agreement (Doc 07) and governs all compensation, invoicing, and payment terms.

## 2. Payment Terms by Tier

| Tier | Payment Terms | Minimum Payout | Method |
|------|--------------|-----------------|--------|
| Micro | Net 30 | $50 | Direct deposit or PayPal |
| Mid-Tier | Net 15 | $50 | Direct deposit or wire |
| Macro | Net 15 | $50 | Direct deposit or wire |
| Celebrity | Per contract | Per contract | Wire transfer |

## 3. Invoicing

- Influencer submits invoices within 5 business days of content publication
- Invoices must include: campaign name, content URLs, deliverable description, and agreed rate
- Company will approve or dispute invoices within 5 business days of receipt

## 4. Revenue Share

- Referral revenue share is calculated monthly based on verified conversions
- Revenue share statements are provided by the 10th of each month
- Minimum payout threshold: $50 (balances below threshold roll to next month)
- Tracking via unique referral codes and UTM parameters

## 5. Bonuses & Incentives

- Performance bonuses paid quarterly based on agreed KPIs
- Venue credits issued within 7 business days of earning
- Event invitations subject to availability and tier eligibility

## 6. Late Payments

- Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month (18% annually)
- Company will notify Influencer of any payment delays within 3 business days
- Influencer may suspend content obligations if payment is overdue by 30+ days

## 7. Clawback Provisions

Company may reclaim payments if:
- Content is found to violate FTC guidelines
- Engagement metrics are discovered to be artificially inflated
- Influencer breaches exclusivity within the clawback period (90 days)

## 8. Tax Obligations

- Influencer is responsible for all applicable taxes
- Company will issue IRS Form 1099-NEC for US-based Influencers earning $600+ annually
- International Influencers must provide applicable tax documentation
