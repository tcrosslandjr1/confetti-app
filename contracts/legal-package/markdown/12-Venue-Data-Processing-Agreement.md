# Confetti Venue Data Processing Agreement

**Effective Date:** May 19, 2026
**Entity:** Confetti App, LLC

---

## 1. Definitions

- **Controller:** Confetti App, LLC (determines purposes and means of processing)
- **Processor:** Venue Partner (processes data on behalf of Controller)
- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable person
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on Personal Data

## 2. Scope of Processing

### Data Subjects
- Confetti users who interact with the Venue through the platform
- Venue staff whose contact information is provided for partnership management

### Types of Personal Data
- User first name (for reservations)
- Reservation date/time, party size, special requests
- Referral source identifier (anonymized)

### Purpose Limitation
Data is processed solely for: fulfilling reservations, providing agreed services, and communicating about active bookings. Venue shall NOT use data for independent marketing without user consent.

## 3. Venue Obligations (as Processor)

- Process data only on documented instructions from Confetti
- Ensure staff with access are bound by confidentiality
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Not engage sub-processors without prior written consent
- Assist Confetti in responding to data subject rights requests within 5 business days
- Assist with DPIA where required
- Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination

## 4. Confetti Obligations (as Controller)

- Provide clear processing instructions
- Ensure lawful basis for data sharing
- Respond to Venue queries about processing requirements
- Conduct periodic compliance reviews

## 5. Breach Notification

Venue must notify Confetti of any confirmed or suspected data breach within 24 hours. Notification must include: nature of breach, categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed.

## 6. Data Transfers

Venue shall not transfer Personal Data outside the country of collection without prior written consent from Confetti and appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent).

## 7. Audit Rights

Confetti may audit Venue's compliance with this Agreement upon 30 days' written notice, no more than once per year. SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification may be accepted in lieu of on-site audit.

## 8. Retention & Deletion

- Active reservation data: Duration of reservation + 30 days
- Completed reservation records: 12 months for dispute resolution
- All Personal Data deleted within 30 days of Agreement termination
- Venue must certify deletion in writing upon request

## 9. Liability

Venue shall indemnify Confetti for any losses arising from Venue's breach of this Agreement or applicable data protection law.

## 10. Term

This Agreement runs concurrent with the Venue Partnership Agreement (Doc 11) and survives termination until all Personal Data is deleted.

## 11. Governing Law

District of Columbia.
