# Confetti Vendor Data Processing Agreement

**Effective Date:** May 19, 2026
**Entity:** Confetti App, LLC

---

## 1. Definitions

- **Controller:** Confetti App, LLC
- **Processor:** The Vendor identified in the signature block
- **Sub-Processor:** Any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data
- **Personal Data / Processing:** As defined under GDPR and applicable data protection laws

## 2. Scope

This DPA applies to all Personal Data processed by Vendor on behalf of Confetti App, LLC in connection with services provided under the underlying service agreement.

### Annex 1: Processing Details
- **Data Subjects:** Confetti users, Influencers, Venue Partners, employees
- **Data Types:** Names, emails, device identifiers, usage data, payment tokens
- **Purpose:** Service delivery as described in the underlying agreement
- **Duration:** Term of the underlying agreement + data deletion period

## 3. Vendor Obligations

- Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Confetti App, LLC
- Ensure authorized personnel are bound by confidentiality
- Implement and maintain security measures including: TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest, access controls, logging, regular vulnerability assessments
- Not engage Sub-Processors without 30 days' prior written notice and equivalent contractual protections
- Assist with data subject rights requests within 5 business days
- Assist with DPIAs where required
- Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance

## 4. Breach Notification

Vendor must notify Confetti App, LLC within 24 hours of becoming aware of any Personal Data breach, including: nature and scope, data subjects affected, consequences, and remedial actions.

## 5. International Transfers

Vendor shall not transfer Personal Data outside the United States or European Economic Area without Standard Contractual Clauses or other approved transfer mechanism.

## 6. Audit Rights

Confetti App, LLC may audit Vendor's compliance annually. SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification may be accepted in lieu of on-site audit.

## 7. Data Return & Deletion

Upon termination, Vendor must return or securely delete all Personal Data within 30 days and certify deletion in writing.

## 8. Liability

Vendor shall indemnify Confetti App, LLC for losses arising from Vendor's breach of this DPA or applicable data protection law.

## 9. Term

Concurrent with the underlying service agreement; survives until all Personal Data is deleted.

## 10. Governing Law

District of Columbia.
