# Confetti DMCA & Copyright Policy

**Effective Date:** May 19, 2026
**Entity:** Confetti App, LLC

---

## 1. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe content on Confetti infringes your copyright, submit a DMCA takedown notice to our Designated Agent with:

1. Your physical or electronic signature
2. Identification of the copyrighted work
3. Identification of the infringing material and its location on Confetti
4. Your contact information (address, phone, email)
5. A statement that you have a good faith belief the use is unauthorized
6. A statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and you are the owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner

## 2. Designated Agent

Confetti App, LLC

Washington, DC

## 3. Response Procedures

Upon receipt of a valid DMCA notice:
- The identified content will be promptly removed or disabled
- The user who posted the content will be notified
- The user may submit a counter-notification (see below)

## 4. Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was wrongly removed, submit a counter-notification with:
1. Your physical or electronic signature
2. Identification of the removed content and its prior location
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that the content was removed by mistake or misidentification
4. Your name, address, phone, and consent to jurisdiction in Washington, DC

## 5. Restoration

If a valid counter-notification is received and the complaining party does not file a court action within 10-14 business days, the content may be restored.

## 6. Repeat Infringers

Confetti will terminate accounts of repeat infringers. Two or more valid DMCA notices against an account will result in permanent termination.

## 7. Misrepresentation

Under 17 U.S.C. Section 512(f), any person who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages.

## 8. Good Faith

Confetti responds to all DMCA notices in good faith and in compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act.
