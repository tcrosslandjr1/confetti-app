# Confetti — Example Itinerary Templates

These are reference-quality examples that define the **voice, specificity, and format** every Confetti boarding pass should match. Each one is modeled after the kind of curated content a local food/lifestyle influencer would post — not a travel guide, not a Yelp list. A text from a well-connected friend.

Use these as quality benchmarks. If a generated itinerary wouldn't make someone screenshot it and send it to a friend, it's not done yet.

## Template 1: The Food Hall Crawl
**City:** DMV (Silver Spring, MD)
**Vibe:** Try a little bit of everything without committing to one spot
**Best for:** Groups, curious eaters, first dates, birthday hangs

```
🎊 VENDOR VILLAGE
✨ One food hall, five vendors, zero FOMO — this is the cheat code for indecisive friend groups

🛫 STOP 1 — WARM-UP
Commas Food Hall · Silver Spring, MD
🔥 Why here: Brand new multi-vendor hall with a full bar on Level 3 — you get the food hall energy with actual cocktails
🍽️ Order this: Watermelon Candyrita + The Commuter (espresso martini) at C3 Bar; jerk nachos from The Champs Kitchen to start
💡 Insider tip: Bar bites are $9.99 and cocktails $10 when paired — that's your happy hour move right there
💵 $25–40/person for drinks + small plates

🎯 STOP 2 — MAIN EVENT
Commas Food Hall (Round 2) · Silver Spring, MD
🔥 Why here: Four different kitchens means four different cuisines — split everything and try it all
🍽️ Order this: Steak tacos from J&J Mex-Taqueria (go Tuesday for $1.95 tacos), shrimp sliders + rum cake from Claude Have Mercy, chicken dumplings from Momo Hub
💡 Insider tip: Tuesday taco nights are a steal — $1.95 each for chicken, chorizo, or steak. Plan accordingly
💵 $20–35/person

🎲 STOP 3 — TWIST MOMENT
Rooftop or nearby bar in the area · Silver Spring
🔥 Why here: Move the party upstairs or down the block for a change of scenery after all that food
🍽️ Order this: Digestif cocktail — something bitter or bubbly to close out the eating portion of the night
💡 Insider tip: Silver Spring has a walkable dining district — just start walking and let the night decide
💵 $15–20/person

🌙 STOP 4 — FINALE
Late-night dessert spot · Silver Spring or DC
🔥 Why here: The night doesn't end at the food hall — find the sweetest close possible
🍽️ Order this: Anything with ice cream or chocolate that requires two forks
💡 Insider tip: Check what's open after 10pm — grab Uber Eats if you end up back home, no shame
💵 $10–15/person

📸 THE MOMENT: The full table spread at Commas — different cuisines from 4 vendors all at once. Post it.
💰 ESTIMATED BUDGET: $65–110 per person
🎯 WHY THIS NIGHT WORKS: You get to be adventurous without committing to one cuisine, one vibe, or one price point. Maximum variety, minimal planning.
```

## Template 2: Rooftop Summer Night
**City:** Northern Virginia (Arlington, VA)
**Vibe:** Warm weather, cocktails with a view, effortless summer energy
**Best for:** Date nights, girls' nights, post-work unwind, summer birthdays

```
🎊 GOLDEN HOUR ASCENT
✨ New rooftop, summer cocktails, and a view that makes every sip taste better

🛫 STOP 1 — WARM-UP
Solset · Arlington, VA
🔥 Why here: Brand new rooftop replacing the original Wilson Hardware — it's the new spot before everyone knows about it
🍽️ Order this: Matcha coco cocktail + jasmine cocktail (get both, share them), strawberry cheesecake for the table
💡 Insider tip: Go early enough to catch the golden hour view — this spot is made for it. The sushi is reportedly excellent too
💵 $18–28/person for drinks + sharable bite

🎯 STOP 2 — MAIN EVENT
[Local NoVa dinner spot with strong cocktail program] · Clarendon or Ballston
🔥 Why here: Settle in for the actual dinner — let the rooftop be the aperitivo, not the meal
🍽️ Order this: Whatever they're known for locally — research ahead or ask the bartender at Solset for their pick
💡 Insider tip: Ask for a patio table if the weather's right — you've already committed to the outdoor summer night
💵 $45–65/person

🎲 STOP 3 — TWIST MOMENT
Rooftop bar hop or hidden cocktail bar · Arlington
🔥 Why here: The best summer nights in NoVa involve at least two rooftops — make it an unofficial tour
🍽️ Order this: A signature craft cocktail you've never seen on a menu before
💡 Insider tip: Clarendon has multiple rooftop options in walking distance — walk until something feels right
💵 $15–22/person

🌙 STOP 4 — FINALE
Late-night bite or dessert bar · Arlington
🔥 Why here: Cap the night with something sweet and unhurried
🍽️ Order this: Whatever looks dangerous on the dessert menu. Commit.
💡 Insider tip: If you drove, the Metro ride back from Ballston to DC is actually a nice close to the night
💵 $12–18/person

📸 THE MOMENT: Golden-hour cocktail photo at Solset with the Arlington skyline behind it. That's the post.
💰 ESTIMATED BUDGET: $90–130 per person
🎯 WHY THIS NIGHT WORKS: You started at the hottest new rooftop before the crowds found it, ate well, and watched the city light up. That's a summer night done right.
```

## Template 3: Industrial Glam Date Night
**City:** Baltimore, MD
**Vibe:** Upscale-casual with architectural atmosphere — dressed up but not stiff
**Best for:** Date nights, anniversaries, "we're doing something real tonight" energy

```
🎊 STEEL & CANDLELIGHT
✨ Industrial architecture meets glam styling — where the building is part of the date

🛫 STOP 1 — WARM-UP
Pre-dinner cocktails at a neighborhood bar · Hampden or Mt. Vernon, Baltimore
🔥 Why here: Warm up before the main event — Baltimore's neighborhoods are underrated for cocktail bars
🍽️ Order this: One classic (old fashioned or Negroni) and one wild card from the specials menu
💡 Insider tip: Hampden is walkable and packed with personality — arrive early and explore before dinner
💵 $15–22/person

🎯 STOP 2 — MAIN EVENT
The Barn & Lodge at The Rotunda · Baltimore
🔥 Why here: The space is genuinely stunning — industrial bones with glam interior styling, plus an iPad cocktail menu that makes ordering feel like an event
🍽️ Order this: Brussels sprouts, fried lobster tail, duck fat fries, and a glass of Poema Cava
💡 Insider tip: The iPad menu means you can browse without a server hovering — use that energy to take your time and order slowly
💵 $55–80/person

🎲 STOP 3 — TWIST MOMENT
Live music or jazz bar · Baltimore
🔥 Why here: After a gorgeous dinner, you want somewhere to sit and actually talk — not a club, not dead silence
🍽️ Order this: Whatever's on tap or a simple wine — this stop is about the music, not the menu
💡 Insider tip: Baltimore has an underappreciated jazz and blues scene. Ask a local or check the venue's calendar before you go
💵 $20–30/person (cover + drinks)

🌙 STOP 4 — FINALE
Late-night waterfront or rooftop · Baltimore Inner Harbor
🔥 Why here: End with the city's best view — the Inner Harbor at night is one of Baltimore's strongest cards
🍽️ Order this: One last cocktail or a nightcap whiskey. Something you sip slowly.
💡 Insider tip: The walk along the harbor after midnight is underrated. Skip the Uber for this one.
💵 $15–20/person

📸 THE MOMENT: The Barn & Lodge interior — the contrast of exposed industrial beams and glam lighting is genuinely photogenic. Take the shot before you sit down.
💰 ESTIMATED BUDGET: $105–150 per person
🎯 WHY THIS NIGHT WORKS: The venue does the heavy lifting aesthetically — you just show up, order the right things, and let the space make the night feel special.
```

**Vibe:** Treat yourself first, then take that energy out
**Best for:** Girls' trips, birthday weekends, "we deserve this" energy, solo treat days

✨ You start at the nail bar and end at the dinner table — fully recharged, fully done up

Nail salon / spa / blowout bar · Your city
🔥 Why here: The night starts before the night — getting ready together IS part of the plan
🍽️ Do this: Book the experience-style treatment, not just a basic manicure. Milk & honey pedicure, CBD massage, whatever sounds most indulgent
💡 Insider tip: Happy hour pricing during off-peak hours (usually weekday afternoons) can get you 10–20% off services — book smart

🎯 STOP 2 — MAIN EVENT
Dinner at a spot with strong cocktails and a vibe that matches how good you look · Best neighborhood in your city
🔥 Why here: You're glowing — pick a restaurant with lighting that knows it

💡 Insider tip: Make a reservation. Seriously. Showing up glam to a 45-minute wait is not the move
💵 $60–90/person

🎲 STOP 3 — TWIST MOMENT
Dessert bar or champagne lounge · City center
🔥 Why here: The vibe transition from dinner to nightlife needs a soft landing
🍽️ Order this: Champagne + something chocolate, or a dessert cocktail you'd normally skip
💡 Insider tip: This is the photo stop. Good lighting, pretty glasses, everyone looking right. Take it here.
💵 $20–35/person

🌙 STOP 4 — FINALE
Rooftop or cocktail lounge · Wherever the night takes you
🔥 Why here: You're fully out now — let the night decide where you end up
🍽️ Order this: Signature cocktail from the menu, whatever the bartender recommends when you say "surprise me"
💡 Insider tip: This stop is unplanned on purpose. The best finale is the one you didn't see coming
💵 $20–30/person

📸 THE MOMENT: The nails + the first cocktail of the night side by side. Classic, iconic, post it.
💰 ESTIMATED BUDGET: $160–275 per person (self-care included)
🎯 WHY THIS NIGHT WORKS: The self-care stop isn't a detour — it's the opening act. By the time you hit dinner, everyone's glowing and the energy is already there.

## Template 5: Culture Day + Dinner
**City:** Washington, DC
**Vibe:** Museums + food + the feeling of actually using your city
**Best for:** Visitors, culture-curious locals, dates that aren't just dinner, weekend afternoons

```
🎊 THE INFORMED EVENING
✨ You did something with your brain today and now you're going to reward it

🛫 STOP 1 — WARM-UP
National Museum of African American History & Culture or Smithsonian of choice · National Mall, DC
🔥 Why here: Free, world-class, and genuinely moving — the kind of place that gives you something to talk about at dinner
🍽️ Do this: Spend 2–3 hours, pick one exhibit to go deep on rather than trying to see everything
💡 Insider tip: NMAAHC requires timed passes — book online before you go. Most other Smithsonians are walk-in
💵 Free (most Smithsonians)

🎯 STOP 2 — MAIN EVENT
Dinner in Shaw, 14th Street, or Navy Yard · DC
🔥 Why here: DC's best restaurant neighborhoods are walkable from the Mall — segue straight from culture to food
🍽️ Order this: Whatever this neighborhood is known for. DC's food scene is serious — ask a local or check Eater DC for the current must-orders
💡 Insider tip: Shaw in particular has some of DC's most interesting independent restaurants — smaller rooms, longer menus, better energy than Penn Quarter tourist spots
💵 $45–70/person

🎲 STOP 3 — TWIST MOMENT
Jazz bar, bookstore bar, or underground cocktail lounge · U Street Corridor
🔥 Why here: U Street is DC's cultural heartbeat — this is where the city's actual nightlife lives
🍽️ Order this: Something local — DC distillery spirits if available, or a house cocktail with a DC story behind it
💡 Insider tip: Bohemian Caverns legacy aside, the U Street corridor still has the best late-night jazz energy in the city
💵 $25–35/person (cover + one drink)

🌙 STOP 4 — FINALE
Rooftop or waterfront close · Georgetown or The Wharf
🔥 Why here: DC's waterfront views are underused — end the night looking at the Potomac
🍽️ Order this: Nightcap cocktail and something light — you already ate, this is the scenic close
💡 Insider tip: The Wharf is walkable from the waterfront Metro stop and has multiple late-night options along the water
💵 $18–28/person

📸 THE MOMENT: The view from The Wharf at night — the Potomac, the bridges, the lights. DC looks best from the water.
💰 ESTIMATED BUDGET: $90–135 per person
🎯 WHY THIS NIGHT WORKS: You're not just going out — you did something. The dinner hits differently when you've already had a conversation worth having.
```

## Tone & Quality Reference

When in doubt, ask: **would a well-followed local food creator post this?**

The bar is:
- **Specific** — names dishes, drinks, days of the week, price callouts
- **Warm** — sounds like a friend, never a bot or a brochure
- **Surprising** — there's always one detail the user didn't expect
- **Visual** — you can picture it before you go
- **Honest about why** — each stop has a reason to exist in the itinerary, not just a slot to fill

If the recommendation could apply to any city or any night, it needs more work.
