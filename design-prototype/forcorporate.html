FAQ.

How does Claude know our company policies?

You set them once in Company Settings → Policies. Per-person cap, alcohol rules, distance from HQ, accessibility requirements. Claude bakes them into every plan and flags violations for approver review.

Where does Claude run? Is our data exposed?

Claude calls go through our backend, not the frontend. Enterprise plans run Anthropic via your private VPC. We never train on your data. SOC 2 Type II, BAA available on Pro+.

How does billing work for outings?

Stripe Connect. We hold deposits, release on completion, and auto-generate receipts in your accounting format (Concur, Expensify, Ramp). Enterprise can route through Workday.

Does it work for multi-city / global teams?

Yes — Pro and Enterprise. Confetti has live partners in 12 cities (NY, SF, LA, Chicago, Austin, Seattle, CDMX, London, Toronto, Berlin, Lisbon, Sydney). Custom city onboarding takes ~2 weeks.

What if our approver is OOO?

Set backup approvers per team. Auto-escalation rules. Threshold-based delegation (team leads can approve under $1K, VPs above).

Can we white-label this?

Enterprise only. We can run Confetti under your brand for internal events. Talk to sales.
