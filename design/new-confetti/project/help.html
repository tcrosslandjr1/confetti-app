how it works · 4 minutes

From a vibe to a printed pass.

You tell Confetti what kind of day you're up for. Sparkle — our AI conductor — routes it to the right specialist agent, books your stops, and prints a pass to your wallet. Show up, check in, share it.

1

Say a vibe.

"date night, brooklyn, $80, walkable" · "crabs in the backyard for 12" · "kids jump-place party under $300" · "rainy library day with two toddlers". Type or pick a chip. Sparkle parses it instantly.

2

Sparkle routes to a specialist.

Night Out · Family · Party · Hosting · Social · Route · Budget — 7 agents working in parallel behind the scenes. You see them in the loader. Powered by Claude, fed by your TikTok taste graph, 50+ city venue DB, and real-time event feeds (Ticketmaster, Yelp, Google Places).

3

Pass prints.

3-stop ticket with venues, timing, route, budget, must-orders, parking, dress code. Apple/Google Wallet pass auto-generated. Tap any stop to flip it and see fetched intel — live crowd, wait times, what to wear, what to order, and a pro tip.

4

Book everything in one tap.

Stripe deposit for the dinner reservation. Ticketmaster for the show. OpenTable for the brunch. All fire in parallel. If one fails, the rest still go through and Sparkle tells you what to do next.

5

Show up.

Geofence auto-detects when you're 1 mi out. The Wallet pass swaps to the next stop's QR. Staff scans it. You're checked in. Confetti points + venue perks fire automatically.

6

Share or save.

Auto-cut 9-second reel posts to TikTok with your watermark. Friends can tap "plan their night" or grab individual stops. Your Trip Passport saves to your scrapbook with stamps, photos, and a receipt.

Things people ask.

What is Confetti?

The AI planner for real life — nights out, family days, kids parties, BBQs, hosting, recharge days. You tell it what you're up for, it builds a 3-stop pass with real venues, timing, route, and budget.

How much does it cost?

Free to try (3 plans/wk). All-Access is $9.99/mo or $99/yr — unlimited plans, Family Mode, Kids Parties, hosting plans, RSVP tracker, Party Memory Kit, boarding passes, rewards. 7-day free trial. Cancel any time from Settings.

What does Sparkle actually do?

Sparkle is the AI conductor (powered by Claude). She parses your intent, routes to the right specialist agent (Night Out / Family / Party / Hosting / Social / Route / Budget), fetches live data from Yelp + Google + Ticketmaster + TikTok, ranks 40+ venues against your taste graph, and composes a pass in under 4 minutes.

How does the TikTok taste graph work?

If you connect TikTok at sign-up, Confetti reads (read-only) your saves, likes, and follows. We classify them locally into vibes (foodie, rooftop, low-key, etc.) and use that to rank venues. We never auto-post. You can see your taste profile in Settings → Your taste graph and delete it any time.

Is my data shared with venues?

Only your booking name + party size + arrival time when you actually book through us. Nothing else. We never sell data to advertisers. We never share TikTok content. See Privacy below.

Can kids use it? Is there parental control?

Family Mode is for adults planning kid-friendly days. Under-13s can't sign up without verified parental consent (COPPA). Kids Mode filters out adult venues automatically. Photos of kids can be auto-blurred. Parents can hide names and locations from reels.

How do bookings work?

One tap fires Stripe (deposits), Ticketmaster (tickets), and OpenTable (free reservations) in parallel. Refund policies follow each venue's rules — usually cancel by 6 PM for deposits, no refunds for tickets, 30 min grace for free reservations.

What if a venue doesn't honor my pass?

Tell Sparkle. Auto-refund within minutes, +100 pts ($1) credit, and we follow up with the venue owner. We re-train hosts. If it happens twice we pull the listing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → Account → Payment methods → Cancel. Or email support@confetti.app. Cancels apply at the end of the current billing period. We never auto-charge after cancellation.

For businesses — how do I get my venue listed?

Visit forcorporate.html or hit /business/claim in the app. We verify via email OTP + license upload. Pre-negotiated rates: 3% per cover delivered. Pre-paid customers, auto-confirmed.

privacy policy

Your data, your call.

Plain English. No dark patterns. We're a small team and we don't sell data.

LAST UPDATED · MAY 24, 2026 · V4.2

What we collect

  • Email + phone — for sign-in and crew check-ins via Twilio. Phone is optional after sign-up.
  • Location — only when you have an active pass. Auto-purged 24h after the night ends. Off by default outside active passes.
  • TikTok saves/likes/follows — read-only, only if you connect TikTok. We classify locally before sending anything to Claude. You can disconnect or delete the graph at any time.
  • Vibe + ranking history — trains the planner agent on what you actually like. You can clear it from Settings → Your taste graph.
  • Payment tokens — held by Stripe; we never see card numbers.
  • Check-in photos — stored on our CDN, encrypted. Auto-deleted after 90 days unless you save them to your scrapbook.

Who we share with

  • Venues you book — just the booking name + party size + estimated arrival.
  • AI providers — OpenAI & Anthropic on contractual no-train terms; prompts are scrubbed before send.
  • Never — no data sold to advertisers. No social-graph crawls. No browser fingerprinting. No third-party analytics that track you off-app.

Your controls

  • Export — a ZIP of every pass, check-in, photo, and AI conversation. Generated in 10 min, delivered to your email.
  • Delete — instant. Tombstones live 30 days for compliance, then gone.
  • Children (under 13) — verified parental consent required. We never knowingly collect from kids without it. COPPA compliant.

Where we're regulated

GDPR (EU) · CCPA (California) · PIPEDA (Canada) · COPPA (US, under 13). Send rights requests to privacy@confetti.app. We respond in < 5 business days.

content consent

Photos, reels, and safety.

What you can post

  • Photos and videos from your own pass and check-ins.
  • Captions, hashtags, and reactions on your own posts.
  • Tags of people who have explicitly opted in via their own Confetti account.

What you can't post

  • Photos of under-13s without verified parental consent (we auto-blur if detected).
  • Photos of identifiable strangers without their consent. We auto-blur unrecognized faces in kids mode.
  • Hate speech, harassment, doxxing, sexually explicit content, violence, IP infringement.
  • Content that names or shames a specific venue staff member.

How we moderate

  • Sightengine + Hive auto-scan every reel and check-in photo before publish.
  • Sparkle AI reviews captions for hate/harassment/PII leaks.
  • Human moderation queue reviews everything auto-flagged within 30 min during NYC business hours.
  • Report any post via the ⋯ menu. We respond in < 24h.

Auto-post safeguards

Confetti never auto-posts to TikTok, Instagram, X, or anywhere else without your explicit review-and-approve. The auto-reel after a night is held for your review until you tap "post".
