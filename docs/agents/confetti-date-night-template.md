# Date Night Template

Canonical 3-stop Date Night plan.

## Flow

1. **Dinner** (food anchor) — seafood/crab house, steakhouse, mezze/tapas, sushi, KBBQ, or hot pot.
2. **Activity** (social anchor, swap-eligible) — lounge, hookah, casino night, jazz/live music, comedy, or dancing.
3. **Nightcap** (moment, swap-eligible) — dessert, tea, cocktails, or a scenic walk.

## Rules

- Default when `occasion = date-night`.
- Stop 1 respects dietary preferences and cultural anchor bias.
- Stop 2 is swap-eligible via partner vote.
- Stop 3 picks scenic-walk when weather is mild and budget is tight; otherwise dessert/cocktail.
- Boosted venues still win within category.
- Max 1 food anchor; nightcap doesn't count toward food quota.
