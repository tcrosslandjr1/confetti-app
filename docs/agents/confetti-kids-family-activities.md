> The master reference for building kids day plans, family plans, birthday plans, and 3-tier itineraries in any major U.S. city. Structured identically to the adult Cultural Activity Universe so AI agents can mix-and-match across both systems.

## PART 1 — Activity Library

### How to Use This Library

Each category contains a table of activities plus **DMV Venue Anchors** — real venues in the Washington DC / Maryland / Virginia metro area. When building itineraries for the DMV, use these anchors. When building for other cities, use them as templates for the type of venue to recommend (e.g., "local trampoline park," "local children's museum").

## PART 3 — Seasonal Calendar

### Spring (March – May)
- Strawberry picking opens at DMV farms (late April)
- Cherry blossom season — National Mall walks, Tidal Basin
- Outdoor adventure season starts — Go Ape, zip lines, bike trails
- Spring break camps at museums and science centers
- Easter egg hunts at farms and parks

### Summer (June – August)
- Waterpark season — SplashDown, Great Wolf Lodge, splash pads
- Monster Jam summer events
- Farm sunflower season — Butler's Orchard Sunflower Spectacular (August)
- Free outdoor movie nights in parks
- Pokémon GO Fest and Community Days (monthly)
- Beach trips — Rehoboth, Ocean City, Sandy Point
- LEGO Discovery Center summer build events
- Camp and outdoor adventure peak season

### Fall (September – November)
- Cox Farms Fall Festival opens (late September)
- Corn mazes, pumpkin patches, apple picking
- Haunted attractions for teens — Field of Screams, Shocktober (October)
- H Street Festival (September) — family-friendly
- MD State Fair Rodeo — mutton bustin' for kids
- Halloween events at farms and amusement parks
- BrickFair LEGO Convention (check dates)

### Winter (December – February)
- Indoor focus — trampoline parks, museums, arcades, bowling
- Monster Jam at Capital One Arena (January)
- IBR Bull Riding events (February–March)
- Indoor waterparks — Great Wolf Lodge year-round
- Ice skating season — Kettler Iceplex, outdoor rinks
- Holiday events — LEGO builds, Pokémon holiday tournaments
- Science center and museum special exhibits
