Confetti Cookie & Tracking Consent Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2026 Entity: Confetti App, LLC

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website or use an application. They help us recognize your device, remember preferences, and understand usage patterns.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

Required for the Service to function. Cannot be disabled. - Authentication and session management - Security tokens (CSRF protection) - Load balancing

2.2 Functional Cookies

Enable enhanced features and personalization. - Language and region preferences - Remembered settings and UI customizations - Recent venue history

2.3 Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how you use the Service. - Page views and feature usage - Session duration and navigation paths - Error tracking and performance monitoring

2.4 Marketing Cookies

Used for targeted advertising and campaign measurement. - Ad platform pixels (with consent) - Attribution and conversion tracking - Social media sharing widgets

3. Other Tracking Technologies

  • Pixels/Web Beacons: Tiny images to track email opens and web activity
  • SDKs: Mobile analytics and crash reporting
  • Device Fingerprinting: We do NOT use browser fingerprinting
  • Local Storage: Used for offline functionality and preferences

4. Consent Management

On your first visit, a cookie banner will present your choices: - Accept All: Enables all cookie categories - Reject Non-Essential: Only strictly necessary cookies - Customize: Choose categories individually

You can change your preferences at any time via Profile > Privacy > Cookie Settings.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies: - Analytics providers (anonymized usage data) - Payment processors (transaction security) - Customer support tools (chat functionality)

Each third party is bound by a Data Processing Agreement.

6. Do Not Track (DNT)

Confetti honors Do Not Track browser signals. When DNT is enabled, we disable non-essential tracking.

7. Cookie Retention

  • Strictly necessary: Session or up to 12 months
  • Functional: Up to 12 months
  • Analytics: Up to 24 months
  • Marketing: Up to 12 months (or until consent withdrawal)

8. Contact

For questions about cookies and tracking: privacy@confetti.app
