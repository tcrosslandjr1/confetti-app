Confetti Subscription & Auto-Renewal Terms

Effective Date: May 19, 2026 Entity: Confetti App, LLC

1. Subscription Plans

Confetti offers the following subscription tiers:

Plan Price Billing Cycle Features
Free $0 N/A Basic recommendations, limited itineraries
Explorer $9.99/mo Monthly Full AI concierge, unlimited itineraries, Taste Graph
Explorer Annual $99.99/yr Annual All Explorer features, 2 months free
VIP $19.99/mo Monthly Priority recommendations, exclusive venues, Party Room

2. Auto-Renewal

Your subscription automatically renews at the end of each billing period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The renewal charge will be at the then-current rate.

3. Free Trial

New users may receive a free trial period. You will not be charged during the trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription will automatically begin, and your payment method will be charged.

4. Cancellation

  • Cancel anytime via Profile > Subscription > Cancel
  • Cancel through the App Store or Google Play (for in-app purchases)
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
  • You retain access to premium features until the period ends

5. Refund Policy

  • Annual subscriptions: Pro-rated refund within 14 days of renewal
  • Monthly subscriptions: No refunds for partial months
  • App Store / Google Play purchases: Subject to their respective refund policies
  • Billing errors: Full refund upon verification

6. Price Changes

We may change subscription prices with at least 30 days' advance notice. Price changes take effect at the next renewal date. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

7. Contact

Billing inquiries: billing@confetti.app
