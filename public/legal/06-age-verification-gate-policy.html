Confetti Age Verification Gate Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2026 Entity: Confetti App, LLC

1. Age Requirements

  • General Use: You must be at least 18 years old to create a Confetti account
  • Alcohol-Related Features: You must be at least 21 years old to access bar recommendations, cocktail menus, or nightlife venues that serve alcohol
  • All Users: Must meet the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction

2. Verification Methods

Confetti implements age verification at the following points:

2.1 Account Registration

  • Date of birth entry (required field)
  • Self-certification checkbox confirming age eligibility
  • Cross-validation against payment method (if available)

2.2 Alcohol-Related Content Gate

  • Secondary age confirmation before accessing 21+ features
  • Persistent toggle stored in user profile
  • Re-verification every 90 days

2.3 Enhanced Verification (When Required)

  • Government-issued ID upload (processed by third-party identity verification provider)
  • ID data is used solely for verification and deleted within 24 hours
  • Required for reported accounts or suspicious activity

3. Compliance

This policy complies with: - Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) - State alcohol advertising regulations - GDPR provisions on children's data (where applicable)

4. Violations

If we discover a minor has created an account: - Account is immediately suspended - All personal data and Taste Graph data are permanently deleted within 24 hours - The minor's guardian is notified (if contact information is available) - The minor is blocked from re-registration for 12 months
