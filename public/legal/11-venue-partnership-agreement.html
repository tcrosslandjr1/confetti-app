Confetti Venue Partnership Agreement

Effective Date: May 19, 2026 Entity: Confetti App, LLC

1. Parties

This Venue Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is between Confetti App, LLC ("Confetti") and the Venue identified in the signature block below ("Venue").

2. Purpose & Scope

Confetti will list the Venue on its AI-powered dining and nightlife recommendation platform. The Venue will be recommended to users based on AI-generated taste matching, location, and contextual signals.

3. Venue Obligations

  • Maintain accurate and current listing information (hours, menus, capacity, photos)
  • Provide updates within 48 hours of any material changes
  • Maintain service standards consistent with the Venue's published offerings
  • Honor any promotions or special offers listed on Confetti
  • Comply with all applicable health, safety, and licensing requirements
  • Designate a Venue Contact for communications

4. Confetti Obligations

  • Accurately represent the Venue based on provided information
  • Provide Venue Partner Dashboard access for analytics and listing management
  • Deliver monthly performance reports (impressions, referrals, bookings)
  • Process payments within agreed terms
  • Protect Venue data per the Venue Data Processing Agreement (Doc 12)

5. Commission & Payment

Referral Type Commission Rate Payment Terms
Direct Booking 10% of booking value Net 30
Walk-In Referral (verified) 5% of average check Net 30
Special Event Referral 15% of event package Net 30
Promotional Feature Flat fee (agreed per campaign) Net 15
  • Minimum payout threshold: $100 (balances roll to next month)
  • Payment via ACH, wire transfer, or check
  • Disputed charges must be raised within 15 business days

6. Data & Analytics

Confetti will share aggregated, anonymized performance data with the Venue, including: - Impression and click-through counts - Referral volume and conversion rates - Anonymized user preference trends relevant to the Venue

Individual user data is NEVER shared. See Venue Data Processing Agreement (Doc 12).

7. Intellectual Property

  • Venue grants Confetti a non-exclusive license to use Venue name, logo, photos, and descriptions for platform listings and marketing
  • Confetti retains all rights to its platform, AI models, and proprietary technology
  • Neither party acquires rights to the other's trademarks beyond this Agreement

8. Term & Termination

  • Initial Term: 12 months from the Effective Date
  • Renewal: Auto-renews for successive 12-month terms unless either party provides 60 days' written notice
  • Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately for material breach (with 30-day cure period for non-payment)
  • Post-Termination: Venue listing removed within 7 business days; final payment within 30 days

9. Representations & Warranties

Each party represents that it has the authority to enter this Agreement and that its performance will not violate any other agreement.

10. Limitation of Liability

Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages. Total liability is capped at the greater of $5,000 or fees paid/payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

11. Dispute Resolution

Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the AAA in Washington, DC. Governing law: District of Columbia.
