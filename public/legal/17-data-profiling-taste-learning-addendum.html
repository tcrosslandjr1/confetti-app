Confetti Data Profiling & Taste Learning Addendum

Addendum to the Confetti Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Effective Date: May 19, 2026 Entity: Confetti App, LLC

This Addendum explains how Confetti uses artificial intelligence and automated profiling to learn your dining, nightlife, and entertainment preferences. It supplements (and is incorporated into) the Confetti Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. What Is Taste Profiling?

Confetti builds a personal Taste Graph for each user. The Taste Graph is an AI-generated model of your preferences across multiple dimensions, including:

  • Cuisine preferences (e.g., Italian, Thai, vegan, comfort food)
  • Flavor profiles (spicy, sweet, savory, umami, adventurous)
  • Ambiance preferences (upscale, casual, intimate, lively, rooftop)
  • Nightlife style (cocktail bars, dance clubs, lounges, live music)
  • Budget tendencies (affordable, mid-range, splurge occasions)
  • Dietary restrictions and allergen information (voluntarily provided)
  • Social preferences (solo dining, date night, groups, celebrations)
  • Time-of-day and day-of-week patterns
  • Geographic preferences and exploration radius

2. How We Build Your Taste Graph

2.1 Explicit Inputs

  • Onboarding mood and preference selections
  • Ratings and reviews you provide
  • Saved venues, wish lists, and favorites
  • Dietary restrictions and allergen declarations
  • Direct feedback to the AI concierge

2.2 Behavioral Signals

  • Venues you view, browse, or click on
  • Itineraries you accept, modify, or skip
  • Time spent viewing venue details
  • Search queries and filter selections
  • Booking and check-in patterns
  • Repeat visits to specific venues or cuisine types

2.3 Contextual Signals

  • Current location (with permission) and time of day
  • Weather and seasonal context
  • Special occasions indicated by you
  • Group composition when using Party Room features

3. AI Processing & Automated Decision-Making

3.1 Taste Agent

Builds and maintains your individual Taste Graph. Processes explicit inputs and behavioral signals to create a multi-dimensional preference model. Updates in near-real-time.

3.2 Recommendation Agent

Uses your Taste Graph to generate personalized venue recommendations, themed itineraries, and surprise-and-delight moments. Applies contextual signals to filter and rank suggestions.

3.3 Group Taste Graph (Party Room)

Merges individual Taste Graphs for group planning. Uses Vibe Vote and conflict resolution algorithms. No member can see another member's full Taste Graph.

4. Automated Decision-Making (GDPR Art. 22)

Our AI recommendations constitute automated profiling. Your rights:

  1. Right to human review: Request that any recommendation be reviewed by a human team member
  2. Right to object: Object to profiling at any time via privacy@confetti.app or in-app settings
  3. Right to explanation: Request meaningful explanation of the logic behind any recommendation
  4. Right to opt out: Disable Taste Graph learning entirely (app provides generic recommendations)

Automated profiling does NOT produce legal effects or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Special Category Data (GDPR Art. 9)

Dietary restrictions and allergen information may reveal health conditions or religious beliefs. We process this data ONLY: - With your explicit consent - For the specific purpose of safe, relevant dining recommendations - Under enhanced security controls (encrypted at rest and in transit)

You may withdraw consent at any time.

6. Data Retention for Taste Data

Data Type Retention Period Deletion Trigger
Taste Graph model Active account + 30 days Account deletion or opt-out
Behavioral signals (raw) 90 days rolling Automatic purge
Explicit preferences Active account duration Account deletion
Group Taste Graph Session + 7 days Session end or member opt-out
Dietary/allergen data Active account duration Consent withdrawal or deletion

7. Your Controls

  • View Taste Graph: Profile > Taste Settings
  • Edit Preferences: Manually override any learned preference
  • Reset Taste Graph: Clear all learned data and start fresh
  • Pause Learning: Temporarily stop AI updates
  • Export Data: Download in JSON format (GDPR Art. 20)
  • Delete All Data: Complete erasure (GDPR Art. 17)

8. Data Portability

Under GDPR Article 20 and CCPA, you may receive your Taste Graph in structured, machine-readable JSON format. Request via privacy@confetti.app or Profile > Privacy > Export My Data.

9. Third-Party Data Sharing

Your Taste Graph data is NEVER sold. Limited, anonymized, aggregated taste trend data may be shared with venue partners. No individual user can be identified from aggregated data.

10. Children & Minors

Confetti does not build Taste Graphs for users under 18. If discovered, the Taste Graph is immediately and permanently deleted.
