Confetti SMS & Push Notification Consent

Effective Date: May 19, 2026 Entity: Confetti App, LLC

1. Consent Categories

By opting in, you consent to receive the following types of notifications:

1.1 Transactional Notifications

  • Booking confirmations and reminders
  • Account security alerts (login, password changes)
  • Payment receipts and subscription updates
  • Reservation changes or cancellations

1.2 AI-Personalized Alerts

  • Taste-matched venue recommendations
  • Event and experience suggestions based on your Taste Graph
  • Limited-time offers from partner venues
  • Group activity notifications (Party Room)

1.3 Marketing Communications

  • New feature announcements
  • Promotional offers and discounts
  • Surveys and feedback requests
  • Confetti newsletter

2. TCPA Compliance

This consent is provided in accordance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. 227). By providing your phone number and opting in: - You consent to receive autodialed and prerecorded messages - Consent is not a condition of purchase - Message and data rates may apply - You may revoke consent at any time

3. Message Frequency

Category Frequency Adjustable
Transactional 2-10 per week No (required for service)
AI-Personalized Up to 5 per week Yes (in-app settings)
Marketing Up to 4 per month Yes (in-app settings)

4. Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out at any time using any of these methods: - SMS: Reply STOP to any message - In-App: Profile > Notifications > Manage Preferences - Email: Send opt-out request to privacy@confetti.app - Opt-out is processed within 24 hours - Transactional messages cannot be fully disabled while your account is active

5. Data Use for Notifications

We use the following data to personalize notifications: - Phone number (for SMS) - Device tokens (for push notifications) - Taste Graph data (for AI-personalized alerts) - Location (for proximity-based suggestions, with permission)

6. Quiet Hours

  • Default quiet hours: 10:00 PM - 9:00 AM (local time)
  • Customizable via Profile > Notifications > Quiet Hours
  • Transactional messages (booking confirmations, security alerts) are exempt from quiet hours

7. Emergency & Critical Alerts

Confetti may send critical notifications outside of your preferences for: - Account security breaches - Safety alerts related to a venue you are visiting - Legal or regulatory requirements - Service-affecting incidents

8. Carrier Compatibility

SMS notifications are supported on all major US carriers. Carrier message and data rates apply. Confetti is not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages due to carrier issues.

9. Consent Records

Your notification consent is logged with timestamp, consent version, and device identifier. Records are retained for the duration of your account plus 5 years for compliance purposes.

10. Modifications

We may modify notification types or frequencies with 30 days' advance notice. Material changes require re-consent.

11. Contact

  • Privacy: privacy@confetti.app
  • SMS Help: Text HELP to our short code
  • Support: support@confetti.app
